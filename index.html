About us

A family-run cottage on the temple street

Sri Ambalavanar Cottage opened on 3 March 2025 on Mayuranathar Sannathi Street, Mayiladuthurai, the street that leads to the temple. Pilgrims and families stay with us for clean rooms, a calm stay and the temple a short walk away.

Ambalavanar is Lord Nataraja, who dances in the golden hall. His dancing form is the emblem of our cottage, and we look after every guest as family.
